# Fabrication Notes

Status: starter process notes, not a shop release.

## Process Order

1. Choose one validation candidate. Default: `DID-V5-A1-BELL-P0`.
2. Make a low-cost prototype or measurement coupon before hardwood work.
3. Measure actual bore ID at regular stations and record the data.
4. Install a removable beeswax mouthpiece and check for leaks.
5. Record cold and warmed-up drone pitch, then trim only from the open foot end.
6. Re-measure pitch and response after every trim.
7. Promote the measured station table into CAD/DXF/design-table authority only
   after the validation rows are complete.

## PVC Prototype Path

Use this when the next question is acoustic length and mouthpiece behavior.

- Cut long using the starter blank length.
- Deburr both ends; the player-contact edge must be smooth.
- Fit a removable wax mouthpiece.
- Record pitch before trimming.
- Trim the foot end in small increments; stop before the target is passed.

## Split-Blank Wood Path

Use this only after the prototype validates length and bore direction.

- Mill oversize stock.
- Split or route halves with clear centerline datums.
- Route the bore profile from a reviewed station table.
- Dry-fit and inspect for leak paths before adhesive.
- Glue with controlled clamping pressure and documented cure time.
- Turn/sand exterior only after the bore is sealed and inspected.

## Stave-Built Path

The stave-built path is the long-term design direction, but it is not V5
starter authority yet. Required upstream work:

- Convert the measured bore profile into inner polygon/stave widths.
- Decide stave count and bevel angle.
- Create a jig plan for repeatable bevel cuts.
- Validate that glue seams do not create air leaks along the bore.

## Safety Notes

- Do not play through uncured adhesive or finish.
- Remove sanding dust from the bore before any mouth contact.
- Treat reclaimed natural stalks as biological materials until cleaned and
  sealed.
- Keep finish claims product-specific and recorded at the time of use.

## Shop Unknowns

- Long straight drilling or routing method.
- Clamping method for full-length split blanks.
- Bore sanding reach.
- Whether a bell flare can be routed repeatably before a dedicated fixture.
- How much trim increment corresponds to cents change for the selected bore.
