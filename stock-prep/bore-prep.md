# Split-and-Glue Bore-Prep Method

Document the full chain for preparing a clean, uniform bore from agave or yucca stalk sections. Covers split, pith removal, and glue-up. This method is needed when the natural interior is too irregular, obstructed by pith, or too small to use as-is.

> **Dust safety:** Agave gouging and sanding releases calcium-oxalate raphides. Wear a P100/N95 respirator, safety glasses, and long sleeves throughout. See `safety/agave-dust.md`.

---

## 1. When to Use This Method

| Stalk type | Bore condition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Agave | Natural hollow, mostly clear, diameter acceptable | Inspect and clean in-place — split not required |
| Agave | Natural hollow but irregular, nodal constrictions, debris | **Split-and-glue** (this method) |
| Yucca | Pithy interior fills the bore | **Split-and-glue required** — yucca has no natural hollow |
| Either | Bore diameter too small for the target instrument | Split-and-glue to gouge a larger bore |

---

## 2. Species Differences

| Property | Agave | Yucca |
|---|---|---|
| Interior | Hollow, fibrous ring at wall, usually open | Dense pith (white spongy core) fills entire bore |
| Pith removal | Minor clean-up of fibrous residue at nodes | Full pith gouge required end-to-end |
| Wall hardness | Harder outer cortex, softer inner layer | Denser and harder; holds shape better after split |
| Splitting tendency | Splits longitudinally along natural grain | Splits cleanly; pith resists uncontrolled cracks |
| Glue-up notes | Seal fibrous inner face before gluing | Pith-free walls bond well; mating faces need to be flat |

---

## 3. Tools and Materials

- **Splitting:** Hand saw or band saw (for a clean kerf split), or a mallet + splitting wedge for a natural grain split on agave
- **Gouging:** Curved gouge (1/2–3/4 in), spoon gouge, or Dremel with drum-sander attachment
- **Sanding:** 60-grit and 120-grit sandpaper or drum-sander
- **Measuring:** Calipers, bore gauge, or a dowel as a go/no-go gauge
- **Adhesive:** Titebond III (waterproof PVA) or epoxy for structural gaps
- **Clamping:** Hose clamps (preferred for round cross-sections), band clamps, or rubber straps; min. 4 clamps per foot of length
- **Sealing (agave):** Thin CA glue or shellac to stabilise fibrous inner face before final glue-up

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Procedure

### Step 1 — Mark the Split Line

1. Identify the natural split axis: agave often has a visible grain line running longitudinally; yucca can be split along any axis.
2. Mark a straight chalk line from end to end along the intended split plane.
3. The split line should pass through the centre of the bore (or estimated pith centre for yucca).

### Step 2 — Split the Stalk

**Option A — Saw split (recommended):** Use a band saw or table saw with the chalk line as a fence guide. Cut in one pass for a flat, clean mating face. Produces two half-shells.

**Option B — Wedge split (agave only):** Score a 1/4-in kerf with a hand saw at each end, then drive a splitting wedge (chisel or wooden froe) along the grain. Faster but less flat; requires more sanding to true the faces.

### Step 3 — Pith Removal

**Agave:** Remove fibrous nodal plugs with a curved gouge. Scrape loose fibre from the inner wall. The hollow should already exist; this step clears debris and constrictions.

**Yucca (full pith gouge):**
1. With the stalk split open, the pithy core is exposed. Use a curved gouge or spoon gouge to remove pith in passes, working from the centre outward.
2. Target bore diameter: match the bore profile for the intended instrument (e.g., 1.5 in mouth ID tapering to 3.5 in bell for a standard didge blank).
3. Leave 1/8–1/4 in of pith against the wall as a reference layer; final dimension is established by sanding, not aggressive gouging.
4. Check diameter at 6-in intervals with calipers as you work.

### Step 4 — True the Bore

1. Sand the interior with 60-grit, then 120-grit, working along the bore axis (not across it).
2. Check roundness: roll a properly-sized dowel or bore gauge through the interior — it should pass without catching.
3. Remove any remaining high spots or pith remnants.
4. For agave: apply a thin coat of thin CA glue or shellac to the fibrous inner surface; let cure fully before gluing up. This prevents glue absorption into the fibre.

### Step 5 — True the Mating Faces

1. Lay each half-shell face-down on a flat surface (a jointer, belt sander, or hand plane on a known-flat reference).
2. Sand or plane the mating faces flat and straight. Check with a straightedge: gap < 0.01 in (no visible daylight) along the full length.
3. A gap here will telegraph into the bore after glue-up.

### Step 6 — Dry Fit

Bring the two half-shells together without glue. Check:
- Mating faces seat fully with hand pressure only
- No twist (ends align)
- Bore profile is symmetric and continuous
- Overall OD is round when closed (no oval cross-section)

Adjust faces if needed before proceeding.

### Step 7 — Glue-Up

1. Apply Titebond III or epoxy to both mating faces — thin, even coat; do not pool into the bore.
2. Align and close the joint. Apply clamps starting from the middle, working outward in alternating directions to prevent cupping.
   - Hose clamps at 4–6 in intervals
   - Snug but not crushing — overtightening distorts the bore
3. Wipe squeeze-out from the bore interior immediately with a damp cloth on a long dowel.
4. Cure time:
   - Titebond III: 24 h open time for repositioning, 24 h clamped, 24 h unclamped before stress
   - Epoxy (if used for large gaps): per manufacturer — typically 24 h to demold, 72 h full cure

### Step 8 — Post-Cure Bore Check

1. Remove clamps after full cure.
2. Check bore diameter end-to-end with calipers at 6-in intervals.
3. Light any surface glue ridges inside the bore with a round file or fine drum-sander.
4. Verify: a suitable bore gauge or dowel passes through the full length without catching.
5. Check the glue line on the exterior: no visible gaps. If gaps exist, fill with CA glue or thin epoxy and re-sand.

---

## 5. Bore Uniformity Acceptance Criteria

| Check | Pass |
|---|---|
| Diameter variation along length | Smooth, predictable taper; no abrupt steps > 1/8 in over 2 in |
| Roundness | ≤ 1/8 in out-of-round at any cross-section |
| Glue-line interior | Flush or < 1/32 in proud; no catching on a bore gauge pass |
| Surface condition | No loose fibres, pith chunks, or debris inside bore |
