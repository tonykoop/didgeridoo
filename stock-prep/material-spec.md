# Agave & Yucca — Material-Selection Spec & Grading Rubric

Use this rubric at harvest or purchase time (A6 log entry) to decide whether a stalk is instrument-grade.  
Grade every stalk before cutting. Record pass/reject + notes in the A6 log.

---

## 1. Dryness

| Check | Pass | Reject |
|---|---|---|
| Weight | Noticeably light for its size — dried stalk feels hollow | Heavy, damp, or noticeably dense |
| Surface | Papery skin, hard outer cortex, no tacky/green zones | Soft or spongy outer cortex; any green colouration |
| Knuckle-tap | Clear ring or hollow knock (see `cure-check.md`) | Dull thud, or muffled — indicates residual moisture |
| Crack pattern | Fine longitudinal cracks acceptable — sign of good drying | Cross-grain or spiral cracks through the wall |

**Reject trigger:** Any single fail in the Reject column = reject the stalk or flag for extended drying.

---

## 2. Wall Thickness

Measure with calipers or a ruler at the cut end and at mid-stalk.

| Measurement | Pass | Reject / Conditional |
|---|---|---|
| Wall thickness (agave) | **1/4 in – 1/2 in** (6–13 mm) | < 1/4 in → too fragile; > 1/2 in → bore too small to gouge reliably |
| Wall thickness (yucca) | **1/4 in – 1/2 in** (same range after pith removal) | Same thresholds; measure post-pith gouge estimate |
| Uniformity around circumference | ≤ 1/8 in variation side to side | > 1/8 in variation → asymmetric bore; mark as conditional |

**Note:** Yucca wall is measured as outer diameter minus estimated pith diameter. If pith is off-centre, the usable wall may be uneven — apply the uniformity check.

---

## 3. Taper

Taper drives backpressure and acoustics. Measure outer diameter at the base end and the upper (mouthpiece) end.

| Characteristic | Pass | Conditional | Reject |
|---|---|---|---|
| Taper direction | Base wider than mouthpiece end (natural bore taper) | Uniform cylinder (no taper) — requires acoustic modelling | Reverse taper (mouthpiece wider than base) |
| Taper rate | Gradual, ≤ 0.5 in diameter change per foot of length | Abrupt shoulder or step > 0.5 in over < 6 in | Kink, crush, or collapsed zone anywhere along the stalk |
| Bell zone | Natural flare or gentle widening at base end | Absent (cylindrical base) — acceptable for rainstick/flute variants | Cracked flare that compromises structural integrity |

**Conditional** stalks may be used for non-backpressure instruments (rainstick, percussion blank) even if they fail the didgeridoo taper spec.

---

## 4. Structural Integrity

| Check | Pass | Reject |
|---|---|---|
| Surface rot | None; dried cortex firm everywhere | Soft spots, black zones, fungal patches |
| Insect damage | Minor surface galleries acceptable | Through-wall galleries; hollow chambers from insect activity |
| Splits | Fine shrinkage cracks (< 1/8 in wide, longitudinal only) | Open splits running through the wall or cross-grain |
| Residue | Light natural wax or surface oxidation acceptable | Pitch, sap, or adhesive residue blocking bore access |

---

## 5. Minimum Useful Length

| Instrument | Minimum stalk length |
|---|---|
| Didgeridoo (A1/Bb1 range) | 55 in (~140 cm) |
| Rainstick | 24 in (60 cm) |
| NA-flute blank | 18 in (45 cm) |
| Trim / test piece | Any length |

Stalks shorter than 55 in that pass all other criteria: log as rainstick/flute candidates.
